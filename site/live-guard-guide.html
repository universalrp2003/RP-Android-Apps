1. Install 3.00

  1. Tap the APK or the ZIP on the app listing. Both contain the same bytes; the ZIP exists because some Android browsers stall on a large .apk download.
  2. Install it over your current Live Guard. Android may ask you to allow installs from your browser — allow it once for that browser, then turn it back off if you prefer.
  3. Your data survives: plan totals, booster details, pill position and size, chosen voices and warning thresholds are all kept.
  4. Open the app and check About shows v3.00. If it shows an older number, the install did not replace the app.

The APK is signed with the same release key as 2.99, so Android accepts it as an update rather than a second app.

2. What changed in 3.00

3.00 is a corrections release. It does not add a new screen; it removes friction from three that already existed.

Pill size moved into Settings

The status pill's size is now adjustable from Settings, where you already go for the pill's SIM display. Two buttons — Smaller and Bigger — with the current percentage shown between them. Previously this lived somewhere less obvious.

The SMS paste-fill card is gone

The card that asked you to paste an operator SMS to fill your plan has been removed. You now type the amount yourself. Fewer steps, and nothing to paste.

History reads the same plan as My plan

These two screens could previously disagree about your remaining data. They now read one stored plan, so the number on History is the number on My plan.

Not in 3.00: no new AI features, no new permissions, and no change to the assistant or the news/weather voice. The 2.99 voice-rotation fix and the rain-headline fixes are carried forward unchanged.

3. The status pill

The pill is the small movable strip that floats above other apps. It shows five readings, in this order:

  1. CPU usage as a percentage.
  2. RAM in use, in MB.
  3. Free storage, in GB.
  4. Battery temperature, in °C.
  5. Network quality as a colour — the LED turns red on a bad connection. That red is a feature, not an error.

Moving it

Drag the pill itself to move it anywhere on screen. If you would rather nudge it than drag, Settings has four arrow buttons, an Auto button that puts it back beside the camera, and a Drag button. The position you leave it in is remembered across restarts.

Pill size

Settings → Pill size → Smaller / Bigger. The percentage updates as you tap and the change applies to the floating pill straight away. Screenshots 3 and 4 above show the same screen at 100% and 90%.

SIM percentage inside the pill

First SIM shows one SIM's percentage. Both SIMs shows each one side by side — never added together. Off hides them. The percentage is a rough estimate derived from Android's own signal scale; it is not a measurement of reception reliability.

The pill needs Android's "display over other apps" permission. Without it the pill cannot appear above other apps.

4. My plan — one screen, nothing else to set

My plan answers one question: how much data is left? It opens with today's usage since midnight and what remains in the pack.

Choose the kind of data

  1. Daily — resets on its own every midnight. Choose this if your operator gives you a fresh allowance each day.
  2. Total — stays used up until you reset it. Choose this for a pack that lasts until your recharge runs out.
  3. No data — voice only. Nothing is counted.

Set the pack size

Preset buttons for 1, 1.5, 2, 3 and 6 GB, plus Custom… for any other amount. Unlimited total data is a separate switch on the same card. The screenshot above shows a 2.2 GB pack, which is the kind of value the Custom field exists for.

Count from now, not from an old date

Start counting from now begins a fresh count at the moment you tap it. Use it right after a recharge. Earlier usage today stays visible on the screen — it is not deleted and it is not added to the new count. Do not add the two numbers together.

Why the app cannot know this by itself. Android reports one mobile-data counter for the whole phone. It does not tell an app when your operator reset your allowance, or how much of a pack was used before the app was installed. That is why you set the pack and press start — the app is being honest about what it can and cannot see, rather than showing a confident number that might be wrong.

Extra data booster

Turn on I recharged an extra data booster, then enter the booster amount and its last day by hand. Nothing is fetched from your operator; you type what you were told.

5. 4G and 5G counted separately

Android keeps a single mobile counter per phone. Turning on Count 4G and 5G separately makes Live Guard sample every three seconds and attribute each sample to whichever radio (4G or 5G) was active at that instant. The two figures appear side by side and are never added together.

Because this is sampled on the phone, the split can only be exact from the moment counting started. Usage from before that point appears in the mobile total instead. The screen says so plainly, in both languages.

5G unlimited on this plan — Auto, On or Off. Set On if 5G does not come out of your data, and 5G usage stops counting against the pack.

Announce 4G ⇄ 5G changes speaks once each time the radio changes, at most every 20 seconds. Quiet hours still apply. Quiet no-signal beep is a separate switch.

6. Cleaning storage

  1. Tap Scan. Live Guard measures junk files, thumbnail caches, duplicate files, leftover APK installers, empty folders, old downloads and large files.
  2. Review the list. Every item is shown before anything is removed.
  3. Remove only what you confirm. Nothing is deleted without your tap, and the app never clears another app's data by itself.
Android 11 and newer: without the file permission Android may only let the scan see certain folders. If a scan reports very little, grant the permission once and scan again — the app will tell you rather than pretending the phone is clean.

7. Troubleshooting

The pill is not visible

Grant Android's "display over other apps" permission for Live Guard. Some phones also need the app excluded from aggressive battery optimisation before the pill survives a reboot.

Pill size changes but the pill looks the same

Close and reopen the floating pill, or toggle the pill off and on in Settings. The value is saved; on a few ROMs the overlay needs to be recreated before the new size is drawn.

Today's usage says a small number after I pressed start

That is correct. The count begins at the moment you pressed Start counting from now. Earlier usage today stays on the screen separately and is not added to it.

The 4G/5G split does not match my operator's app

The split is sampled on the phone in three-second steps and begins only when you enable it. Your operator may count differently — partly on 5G, partly on 4G, or before you started. Treat the split as a guide, not as billing.

A reading says unavailable

Some phones do not expose every sensor or counter. Live Guard shows "unavailable" instead of guessing a number. This is deliberate.

History and My plan disagreed

That was the 3.00 bug and it is fixed. If you still see two different numbers, you are likely running an older version — check About shows v3.00.

8. Privacy and trust boundaries

Scanning, cleaning, health readings, the security review and the assistant all run on this phone. The internet is used for exactly two things you start yourself: the optional AI analysis and the speed test. Nothing is uploaded unless you tap Analyse.

Live Guard does not read other apps' messages or notifications. The Accessibility service was dropped in v1.3 and stays gone. The red LED on the pill reflects your own network quality and is not a report about anything else.

The source is available for inspection, but this is not an independent security audit, a Play Protect clearance, or a guarantee that every ROM reports every sensor. What the owner reported and what automated tests covered are different scopes, and this page does not blur them.

மிதப்புப் பட்டி

பட்டி ஐந்து அளவீடுகளைக் காட்டும்: CPU சதவீதம், RAM (MB), காலி சேமிப்பு (GB), பேட்டரி வெப்பநிலை (°C), நெட்வொர்க் தரம் நிறமாக. இணைப்பு மோசமாகும்போது விளக்கு சிவப்பாகும் — அது தேவையான அம்சம், பிழை அல்ல. பட்டியை இழுத்து எங்கும் நகர்த்தலாம்; அமைப்புகளில் நான்கு அம்புப் பொத்தான்கள், தானியங்கி மற்றும் இழு உள்ளன. நீங்கள் விட்ட இடம் நினைவில் இருக்கும்.

எனது திட்டம்

தினசரி — நள்ளிரவில் தானாக புதுப்பிக்கும். மொத்தம் — நீங்கள் மீட்டமைக்கும் வரை அப்படியே இருக்கும். தரவு இல்லை — குரல் மட்டும். தொகுப்பு அளவுக்கு 1, 1.5, 2, 3, 6 GB பொத்தான்கள் மற்றும் விருப்ப அளவு… உள்ளன. இப்போதிலிருந்து எண்ணத் தொடங்கு என்பதை ரீசார்ஜ் செய்த உடனே அழுத்துங்கள். அதற்கு முந்தைய பயன்பாடு திரையில் தனியாகவே இருக்கும்; அதைக் கூட்ட வேண்டாம். Android ஒரு கைப்பேசிக்கு ஒரே மொபைல் கவுண்டரை மட்டுமே தருகிறது; உங்கள் இயக்குநர் எப்போது அளவை மீட்டமைத்தார் என்று அது செயலிக்குத் தெரிவிப்பதில்லை. அதனால்தான் அளவை நீங்கள் அமைத்துத் தொடங்க வேண்டும்.

4G, 5G தனித்தனி எண்ணிக்கை
