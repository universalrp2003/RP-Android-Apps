# Android TV Guard screenshots

Ten screens captured with the app's own **Take Screenshot** button on the owner's Sony Bravia,
1920×1080 PNG, English interface, on 11 November 2026 from the 1.5 build.

- `5.png` is the Tools & Settings overview showing the five-reading status strip.
- `1.png`, `2.png` are the Expert switch lists.
- `9.png` is the home grid; `10.png` the debloater review list.
- `7.png`, `8.png` are the hardware evidence screens, including the physical-disk comparison.
- `6.png` is the Wi-Fi hub file list showing screenshots and installable APKs.
- `3.png`, `4.png` are the hub page as seen in a phone browser (1920×1080 phone screenshots
  supplied separately, downscaled by the browser; they are not used on the site card).

Review notes, per this repository's image policy:

- These are real app screens, not illustrations.
- No account names, messages, OTPs, phone numbers, addresses or financial data appear.
- They are not a security certification and must not be relabelled as one.
- They show this TV's own settings, not the app's defaults.
- Tamil screens are produced by switching the app language; they are not included yet, because
  a translation review is still outstanding.
