**Ramesh Radio 1.17** brings Live Guard's proven **30-Minute Rain, Heavy Downpour & 24×7 Cyclone Voice Alert** system directly into your radio & local music player — with **ZERO Android Location permissions added**:

4. **Compact Floating Rain / Lightning / Cyclone Animation + Free Tomorrow.io Key Guide:**
   - Animated mini strip appears at the top of Ramesh Radio only during active rain/storm alerts (with a **Preview animation** button inside Settings), alongside a direct free **Tomorrow.io API key** link and bilingual voice guide.
