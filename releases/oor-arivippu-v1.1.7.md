## Oor Arivippu (ஊர் அறிவிப்பு) v1.1.7 — Compact Clean Home Screen, Auto HD Voices & Travel Distance Tracker

**Package:** `com.oorarivippu.voiceannouncer` · **Version:** `1.1.7` (`versionCode = 12`) · **Minimum Android:** Android 8.0+ (API 26)

### What's New in v1.1.7
- **Redesigned Ultra-Clean Home Screen:**
  - **Top Header Bar:** App name on the left + one-tap **`தமிழ் | ENG`** pill (switches both UI language and voice announcement language simultaneously) + **`⚙️ Settings`** button on the right (all detailed settings neatly organized inside Settings).
  - **Big Primary `தொடங்கு / நிறுத்து` (`Start / Stop`) Button** right at the top.
  - **Conditional Rain Alert Banner:** Appears **only** when rain or storm is genuinely forecast for your current area — zero stale warnings!
  - **Current City / Place Card:** Clean display of current village/area, taluk/district, GPS accuracy, and update time.
  - **Current Session Traveled Distance (`km`) Box:** Real-time noise-filtered trip odometer (`0.00 km`) that resets automatically whenever you press Start (`தொடங்கு`).
  - **Destination Box:** Set or clear your target destination right on Home to track live remaining distance and receive automatic arrival voice announcements.
- **Automatic HD Studio Online + Offline System Male/Female Voices:**
  - Automatically rotates crisp Studio Neural Male ↔ Female voices online and falls back seamlessly to matching Male/Female Android System TTS offline — zero complicated voice menus!
- **Strictly Scoped Active Rain Alerts:**
  - Checks rain forecasts **only** while the announcer is running (`தொடங்கு` ON) or while the app window is open on screen.
- **Updated Bilingual Guide:** Full Tamil & English in-app guide updated for the new layout and controls.
