# Elderly Launcher 1.8 — contacts and Home

- Favourite contacts use aligned buttons and remove controls, including wrapped names. Large text uses a single column.
- The Android Home button returns from All apps to the home screen. Existing contact favourites and the Home role were retained in an Android 15 emulator upgrade from the public 1.7 APK.
- The signed APK was tested on an emulator, including synthetic Tamil names, 200% text and removal. This exact version was not tested on a physical phone; behaviour can vary by device. No new permissions were added.
- Update normally without uninstalling or clearing data; uninstalling can erase local data.
