# Oor Arivippu 1.1.10 — fresh-install signing migration

**Version:** 1.1.10 · **versionCode:** 15 · **Package:** `com.oorarivippu.voiceannouncer`.

This release preserves Oor Arivippu 1.1.9's place/destination announcement behavior. The Android signing identity changes because the former signing key was exposed; the app's update check remains read-only and opens the official website in the browser.

**Install warning:** Android cannot update an old-key installation with this APK. Download this official APK first, export/back up anything important, then uninstall the old app and install this version through Android. Uninstalling may erase local data. Do not uninstall before obtaining the replacement APK. Stop if Play Protect warns; do not bypass it.

The exact replacement-key APK was not installed on a phone. CI verification is not a device test or Play Protect clearance. The previous 1.1.9 tag and asset remain unchanged.
