# Live Guard 2.58 (`versionCode = 63`) — Auto-Enable Rain Alert on GPS, Mini Live Alert Animation & Free Tomorrow.io Key Guide

## English Summary

Building directly on the physical-device verification of **Live Guard 2.56 & 2.57** on **OPPO A3s** and **Redmi 13 5G**, **Live Guard 2.58** brings three requested upgrades while adding **zero Android permissions**:

1. **Automatic Rain & Storm Watch Activation When Phone GPS Is ON:**
   - On installing/updating when device GPS is ON (and unless manually turned OFF by you), Live Guard automatically turns **Rain & storm alerts** ON and arms background weather & cyclone monitoring immediately.
2. **Compact Floating Rain / Storm / Cyclone Animation Near Live News:**
   - When ~30-minute rain, heavy downpour, or a cyclone warning is active, a sleek animated strip floats right below **Live News** on Home — showing smooth falling raindrops (`~30 min`), heavy rain streaks with lightning flashes, or a spinning cyclone vortex icon.
   - **100% invisible on dry normal days** so your Home screen stays clean, plus an interactive **Preview animation** button inside the Weather screen.
3. **Free `Tomorrow.io` API Key Official Link & Step-by-Step Voice Guide:**
   - Inside **Tomorrow.io 1-minute precision (optional)**, tap **Get free Tomorrow.io API key — official site** (`https://app.tomorrow.io/development/keys`), open the docs link, or open the **Step-by-step setup & voice guide** (`Read guide aloud` / `Stop voice`) — matching the AI & VirusTotal API key guides.
   - Keys remain encrypted locally via Android Keystore (`AES-GCM`), and all Sister App links (`Ramesh Radio 1.17`, `Oor Arivippu 1.1.3`) are updated together.
