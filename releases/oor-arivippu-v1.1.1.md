# Oor Arivippu 1.1.1 — Stable

A guide inside the app, in Tamil and English.

## Highlights

- **New — About → Guide.** Short questions that open when tapped: how announcements work, what each trigger does, why a phone may hold a voice until a call ends, how to change the voice speed, and what the app does not do.
- Nothing about the voice engine, triggers or announcements changed in this release.
- There is no new Android permission.

## Installation and safety

Download `Oor-Arivippu-v1.1.1.apk` and install it through Android. If Android asks, enable **Allow from this source** for the browser or file manager you used, then confirm in Android's installer. No silent installation and no Play Protect bypass is used.
