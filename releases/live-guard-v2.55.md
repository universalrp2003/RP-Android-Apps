# Live Guard 2.55

A small Guide button is now on Home, and the in-app guide has clearer Tamil and English instructions for the main tools.

## What changed

- **Guide on Home:** tap the book/help button in the top row to open the matching Live Guard guide directly. Its topics expand when tapped and cover navigation, storage scans, battery and heat readings, data and plan setup, voice notices, privacy limits, updates and the other RP Ideas apps.
- The guide explains that scans are reviewed before anything is removed, sensor values may be unavailable on some phones, and plan warnings use the values you entered.
- No new Android permission. App behavior outside the guide entry point is unchanged.

## Verification and device testing

The exact APK was **not installed on a phone before publication**. Build, unit/regression, localization, APK and release-asset checks are recorded by the publishing workflow. Passing CI is not a Play Protect approval or an all-device compatibility claim.

Version 2.55 / code 60.
