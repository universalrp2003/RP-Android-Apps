# Live Guard 2.87 — disaster reports within news

- Adds a separate **Disaster updates with news** opt-in under Voice → News. Screen-only remains the default until enabled. No standalone disaster alarm or new permission.
- A nearby GDACS cyclone, flood or earthquake is included once in each of the next **three fully completed news bulletins**, not three times together. Failed synthesis, Stop, interruptions and preparation do not use a slot. Event/report updates, day, language and home edits do not reset its count.
- Uses an enabled, permitted, recent phone fix when available; otherwise explicitly says **from your saved home**. If neither exists, there is no disaster speech. Distances are approximate to a dated reported position/reference point, not impact areas or live tracking.
- Cyclone wind uses the dated **observed/current advisory** sustained wind and documented GDACS units, not a forecast or overall maximum or local wind. Missing data says unavailable. Movement remains unavailable where no verified provider unit/value contract exists; no guessed speed or zero-as-stationary claim.
