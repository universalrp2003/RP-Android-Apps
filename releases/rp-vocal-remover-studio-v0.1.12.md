# RP Vocal Remover Studio 0.1.12

A guide inside the app, in Tamil and English.

## Highlights

- **New — About → Guide.** Short questions that open when tapped: what the vocal reduction does, how to export WAV or MP3, why a long song needs time, and what the app does not do yet (there is still no seek bar).
- No processing or export behaviour changed in this release.
- There is no new Android permission.

## Installation and safety

Download `RP-Vocal-Remover-Studio-v0.1.12.apk` and install it through Android. If Android asks, enable **Allow from this source** for the browser or file manager you used, then confirm in Android's installer. No silent installation and no Play Protect bypass is used.
