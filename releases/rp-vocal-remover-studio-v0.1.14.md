# RP Vocal Remover Studio 0.1.14

The main screen now opens the full Tamil/English guide directly.

## What's new

- **Guide button on Home:** tap the small help icon in the header. The expandable guide covers the first model download, offline use afterwards, processing-speed choices, WAV and optional MP3 output, playback limits, queues, safety pauses, privacy and updates.
- The guide clarifies that results are saved as separate files, the original is preserved, and extra reduction can also soften instruments.
- No new Android permission. Model and audio-processing behavior is unchanged.

## Verification and device testing

The exact APK was not installed on a phone before publication. Build, unit, localization, lint, APK and release-asset checks are recorded by the publishing workflow. CI is not a device-compatibility certification.
