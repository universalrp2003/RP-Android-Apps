# Oor Arivippu 1.1.5 (`versionCode = 10`) — 10 Studio Ultra-HD Online Travel Voices + Daily Auto Male ↔ Female Rotation (Turned ON by Default)

## English Summary

Upgrading travel voice clarity across every village, town, district, landmark, destination, and rain/storm alert with **zero new Android permissions**, **Oor Arivippu 1.1.5** introduces **10 Studio Ultra-HD Online Neural Voices (`5 Male HD + 5 Female HD`)** turned **ON by default**:

1. **10 Studio Ultra-HD Travel Voices (`5 Male HD + 5 Female HD`, Tamil & English):**
   - Includes all four official world Tamil neural studios (`ta-IN-ValluvarNeural`, `ta-LK-KumarNeural`, `ta-SG-AnbuNeural`, `ta-MY-SuryaNeural`, `ta-IN-PallaviNeural`, `ta-MY-KaniNeural`, `ta-SG-VenbaNeural`, `ta-LK-SaranyaNeural`) plus studio bass & clear road-travel prosody profiles and 10 flagship Indian, Multilingual & Expressive English neural voices.
2. **Turned ON by Default Out-of-the-Box & on Update (`1.1.4 -> 1.1.5`):**
   - Studio Ultra-HD Online Voice and **Daily Auto Male ↔ Female Rotation** automatically start **ON** out of the box (and migrate ON once when updating from `<= 1.1.4`), keeping multi-day bike, car, and bus journeys fresh every day while preserving manual overrides.
3. **0 ms Local Town Cache + Instant Offline Android System TTS Fallback:**
   - Caches spoken village/town/district MP3 clips on device for instant `0 ms` playback on return trips, respects Slow / Normal / Fast speed & volume settings, pauses safely during active phone calls, and automatically falls back to your phone's installed Android `TextToSpeech` system voice whenever internet drops.
4. **Interactive Studio Voice Card on the Home Screen:**
   - Shows **Today's** and **Tomorrow's** scheduled voices, **`👨 Preview Male HD`** & **`👩 Preview Female HD`** buttons, and all **10 Studio Ultra-HD Voices** for instant one-tap preview or locking.
