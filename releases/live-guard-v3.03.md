# Live Guard 3.03 candidate

When a charger disconnect is observed, Live Guard can announce the battery levels at the observed connection and disconnection, their net change in percentage points, and the time since the observed connection. Missed power events can make a session incomplete. Missing or uncertain values are not guessed. The existing voice switch, quiet/silent-place, call and connectivity rules still apply; Android or device power management may delay or miss background events. Battery percentage change is not a measure of energy delivered. Battery, charging, temperature, storage and network wording is clearer; estimates are identified and unrecognized Wi-Fi devices are described as detected, not proven intruders.

Candidate only: update in place without uninstalling or clearing local data. Uninstalling may erase local data. It is not installed on a phone.
