# Live Guard 3.04

Shorter, clearer Tamil and English voice alerts for charging, battery, Wi-Fi and other phone checks. When a charger is disconnected, Live Guard can announce the observed starting and ending battery levels, elapsed time and net change. It does not invent missing readings. Weather remains a forecast and battery health an estimate. Settings, connectivity and Android background restrictions can affect whether announcements are heard.

Update in place without uninstalling or clearing data; uninstalling may erase local data. The exact signed APK passed automated and Android emulator upgrade checks. No device test of physical-phone installation and audible playback of these exact bytes has been independently confirmed.
