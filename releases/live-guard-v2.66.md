### What's New in v2.66
- **Rich "Rain details" window (tap the rain notification → opens the Rain & Storm screen):** a fully data-driven panel (no radar map, no extra battery drain) that shows every accurate detail the forecast already holds:
  - **Current conditions** — temperature, cloud cover, wind gust, and rain-now rate.
  - **"Next precipitation" 2-hour chart** — per-10-minute intensity bars colour-graded from light to very heavy, with the peak rate called out.
  - **A plain-language headline** — "Rain will start in about X min", "Raining now", or "No rain in the next 2 hours".
  - **Active cyclone / storm banner** — official advisory name, alert level (Red/Orange), distance and maximum wind whenever a severe-storm warning is in force for your area.
- **Cyclone & very-heavy-rain safety lead in the News bulletin:** when a Very Heavy Rain or Cyclone warning is active for your location, the spoken news bulletin now **opens with a bilingual life-safety warning** (identical wording to the standalone weather alert), on both the automatic screen-OFF bulletin and the manual **Read today's news**.
- **Latest cyber-fraud headlines (Indian / Tamil Nadu):** the news strip and bulletin now carry up-to-date cyber-fraud / online-scam coverage — UPI & OTP scams, fake job offers, digital-arrest calls and similar — in **Tamil and English**, so you hear how people are being cheated right now.
- **Zero new permissions** over `v2.65`; the package name, verified updater and signing key are unchanged.
