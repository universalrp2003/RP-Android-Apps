# Live Guard 3.02 — review fixes

This update addresses the Live Guard review: news voice rotation advances only after playback starts, and completed-story counts do not reinterpret old history. It improves weather freshness, Wi-Fi scan-state wording, English and Tamil copy, and large-text home controls. App review now treats missing usage history and installer identities as unknown rather than proof of inactivity or sideloading. App-storage totals no longer count cache twice and clearly identify incomplete readings. Package-name matches are informational, not a reason to disable an app.

Update over the existing app without uninstalling or clearing data; uninstalling may erase local data. Android still controls permissions and background behavior; a security-review score is not proof that a phone is safe. The owner reported that the preceding 3.01 review candidate worked on their phone and authorized publication; the installed APK was not independently hash-checked. Version 3.02 is a version-only rebuild so Android can distinguish this update from the previously published 3.01; these exact 3.02 bytes have not been tested on a physical phone. CI checks and disposable-emulator upgrades are described in the publication receipt.
