# Ramesh Radio 1.29 — fresh-install signing migration

**Version:** 1.29 · **versionCode:** 29 · **Package:** unchanged.

This release keeps the Ramesh Radio 1.28 app behavior and changes the Android signing identity because the former signing key was exposed. No radio, station, permission or playback behavior is intentionally changed.

**Install warning:** Android cannot update an old-key installation with this APK. Download this official APK first, export/back up anything important, then uninstall the old app and install this version through Android. Uninstalling may erase local data. Do not uninstall before obtaining the replacement APK. Stop if Play Protect warns; do not bypass it.

The owner tested the 1.28 compact-layout candidate, not this new-key 1.29 APK. The exact replacement-key APK was not installed on a phone. CI verification is not a device test or Play Protect clearance. The previous 1.28 tag and asset remain unchanged.

Play Protect எச்சரித்தால் நிறுத்துங்கள்; அதைத் தவிர்க்க வேண்டாம்.
