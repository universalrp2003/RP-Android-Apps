# Ramesh Radio 1.15

The first screen now shows the stations, not paragraphs.

## Highlights

### Less to read before you press play

- **Categories:** the six that matter open first — **தமிழ் · Tamil FM · Tamil News · Towns ·
  Favourites · Recent** — and the genre/archive chips sit behind **More ▾**, which expands in place
  and turns into **Less ▴**. Fourteen chips no longer push the list down.
- **Search:** one line until you tap it (already in 1.14) and it stays open once you have typed.
- **Sorting note:** folded into **How this list is sorted ▾**.
- **Empty state:** the action (Search the directory) comes first; the explanation is one short line
  under it instead of a paragraph above the button.
- **Header:** the subtitle is one line and ellipsised, so a long station name or a big Tamil word
  cannot push the data pill off the screen.

### Tamil that fits

- The data pill used to print **இன்று** even when the app was in English. The word now follows the
  language; the number is unchanged.
- Bottom-bar labels are one line, no wrapping, centred with a little breathing room, so **செய்திகள்**
  and **பாடல்கள்** sit cleanly instead of clipping at the edges.

## Not in this build yet

The home-screen **widget chooser** (Tamil FM / Tamil news / English news / last station) and the
larger visual restyle are the next pass, 1.16. Nothing here changes playback, favourites, the
equalizer or the widget's existing play/pause/skip buttons.

## Installation and safety

Download `Ramesh-Radio-v1.15.apk` and install it through Android. It installs over 1.14 with the same
signing key; favourites, custom stations, the equalizer and the language choice are kept. No new
permission is added.

**Device testing:** this build was **not installed on a phone** at the time of writing. It passed the
CI build, the radio unit tests, Android lint and the APK checks. That is not Play Protect approval and
not an all-device compatibility claim.
