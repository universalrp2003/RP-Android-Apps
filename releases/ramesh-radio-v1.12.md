# Ramesh Radio 1.12

A guide inside the app, in Tamil and English.

## Highlights

- **New — About → Guide.** Short questions that open when tapped: how to play a station, what the saved list does, why a stream can pause, what the session boost is, and what the app does not do. Nothing important is hidden in a wall of text.
- No audio, playlist or notification behaviour changed in this release.
- There is no new Android permission.

## Installation and safety

Download `Ramesh-Radio-v1.12.apk` and install it through Android. If Android asks, enable **Allow from this source** for the browser or file manager you used, then confirm in Android's installer. No silent installation and no Play Protect bypass is used.
