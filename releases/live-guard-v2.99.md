# Live Guard 2.99 — persistent HD voice rotation

Random voice mode now retains its male/female HD voice rotation across Android process restarts. One voice is retained throughout each bulletin; the next announcement takes the next turn. Fixed male-only and female-only modes remain unchanged. A failed voice attempt may consume a rotation turn, but not a news-story completion checkpoint. HD provider fallback may still change the actual voice; no audible gender guarantee is claimed.
