# PulseEQ 1.8

The main screen now has a direct route to a clearer bilingual guide.

## What's new

- **Guide button on the equalizer home screen:** tap the small help icon in the header. The expandable Tamil/English guide covers choosing and playing a file, band controls, presets, reset and bypass, the spectrum, device limits and updates.
- The guide now explains that PulseEQ does not provide seek-in-file controls and that changing other apps' sound depends on the phone.
- No change to audio processing and no new Android permission.

## Verification and device testing

The exact APK was not installed on a phone before publication. Build, unit, localization, lint, APK and release-asset checks are recorded by the publishing workflow. CI is not a device-compatibility certification.

Version 1.8 / code 9.
