# Ramesh Radio 1.16

Find help from the station screen, and seek through local songs while they play.

## What's new

- **Guide button on the main station screen:** tap the small help icon beside the header. The bilingual, expandable guide opens directly and explains live stations, local files, news pages, sound controls, favourites, custom stations, sleep timer and updates.
- **Seek a local file:** while a local audio file is playing, the Local screen now shows its position bar. Drag it to move to another point in the recording; elapsed and total time remain available in Now Playing. This is for files with a readable duration. Live radio is still limited to the short buffered window and has no full-track seek.
- No new Android permission. Local folder access still uses Android's document picker; the app does not scan the whole phone.

## Verification and device testing

The exact APK was not installed on a phone before publication. Build, radio unit tests, localization, lint, APK and release-asset checks are recorded by the publishing workflow. CI is not a device-compatibility certification.
