## English Summary

**Oor Arivippu 1.1.3** adds full **30-Minute Approaching Rain, Heavy Downpour & 24×7 Cyclone Voice Alerts** built seamlessly around Oor Arivippu's travel voice architecture — with **zero new Android permissions added**:

1. **Auto-Enable When Phone GPS Is ON + Smart Travel GPS / PIN Fallback (`622301`):**
   - Automatically enables Rain & Cyclone Watch on update when phone GPS is ON.
   - Uses live phone GPS automatically while you travel on bike/car/bus, and seamlessly falls back to your saved PIN/village (`622301`, `alangudi 622301`) or saved destination whenever GPS is turned OFF!
2. **Quiet Hours Respected for Normal Rain — 24×7 No-Skip Safety for Heavy Rain & Cyclones:**
   - Zero routine daily weather noise. Normal ~30-minute rain heads-up stays silent during Quiet Hours (**10:00 PM – 7:00 AM**), while **very heavy rain and cyclone alerts announce 24×7 day or night** (never interrupting active phone calls).
3. **Compact Floating Rain / Storm / Cyclone Animation + Free Tomorrow.io API Key Guide:**
   - Animated mini raindrop / lightning / spinning cyclone banner right below the top header during active alerts (hidden on calm days, with **Preview animation** inside settings), plus direct official link & bilingual voice setup guide for a free personal **Tomorrow.io** 1-minute radar key (encrypted with Android Keystore).
