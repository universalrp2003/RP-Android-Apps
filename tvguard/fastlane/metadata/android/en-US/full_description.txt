Android TV Guard is a free, open-source cleanup and launcher app for Android TV and Google TV boxes, built for a remote, not a touchscreen. No account, no ads, no analytics.

REMOVE THE JUNK THAT SHIPPED WITH YOUR BOX
Cheap Android TV boxes arrive with things you never asked for: pirate streaming players, third-party app stores, ad-heavy installers, and vendor telemetry or updater services. They eat the box's small storage and memory and make every screen slower. TV Guard lists what is installed and sorts it by risk: SAFE TO REMOVE (known pirate streamers, third-party stores, ad installers, vendor telemetry), YOUR CALL (browsers, extra launchers, file managers), and PROTECTED CORE (system UI, TV settings, Play services and the keyboard are never offered for removal). On a rooted box you remove a choice with one OK press. On the usual unrooted box, TV Guard shows the exact adb command to send from a phone or PC. Nothing is ever removed without a confirmation, so a wrong tap cannot uninstall anything.

A CLEAN, LIGHT LAUNCHER
Set TV Guard as your Home app and the box no longer boots into the vendor home screen with its promo row and tiny targets. You get real app icons, favourite apps first, large tiles, and a focus ring that is always visible from the sofa. Hide the apps you never open; Tools lists every installed app with a tick box. Extras start switched off, so the launcher itself stays light, and you turn on only what you want.

FILE SHARING AND PHONE REMOTE OVER WI-FI
Turn on the hub and open the address it shows in any browser on a phone on the same Wi-Fi. No app on the phone, no cable, no USB stick, no second file manager. Drag an APK or any file onto the TV and install it from the TV's own list. Browse and download the TV's screenshots. Use the phone as a remote: type into TV search boxes, send direction and OK keys, take a screenshot, or free memory. It is off by default, runs only while you leave it on, and only on your own network. It has no password or encryption, so use it on a network you trust and switch it off afterwards.

FIVE READINGS ON ONE MOVABLE LINE
An optional status strip shows processor load, free RAM, free storage, temperature and internet quality as a colour, over whatever is playing. One short line with no panel behind it. Move it with the remote. Anything that cannot be measured says so instead of guessing.

HONEST STORAGE
Many boxes report more free space than the chip can hold. TV Guard cross-checks the visible figure against the physical disk and never shows more free space than the device physically has. The hardware screen shows both numbers side by side and explains the difference.

HARDWARE TRUTH CHECK
Reports what Android says, what the Linux kernel says, and where they disagree: RAM, storage, Android version, boot properties and root status. It is a truth check, not an antivirus: it cannot see inside other apps, and it cannot force dishonest firmware to tell the truth — it can show you that the firmware disagrees with itself.

ENGLISH AND TAMIL
Every label and message exists in both languages. On Android 13 and later you can switch the app language from system settings.

PRIVACY
No account, no analytics, no ads. The Wi-Fi hub runs only while you switch it on, on your own network. Hardware readings stay on the device.

Requirements: Android TV 8.0 or later. Removing preinstalled apps requires root or wireless ADB, which Android itself demands; without either, TV Guard can only open the standard uninstall dialog for apps you installed yourself. Some readings, such as temperature, depend on what your TV exposes to apps, and the app says so when a reading is unavailable. Not verified on every TV model, and not a Google Play Protect clearance.
