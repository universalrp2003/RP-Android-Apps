# Animal communication: research and prototype decisions

Scope: evidence review for an experimental Android app, not a claim to have surveyed all work worldwide. Source observations are not proof that a model works on the owner's device, in Tamil Nadu, for a particular animal, or across noise, dialects and individual variation.

## What science can and cannot do

1. [NatureLM-audio research paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.07186v2) evaluates diverse bioacoustic recognition and free-text tasks; performance varies dramatically between datasets and labels. It does not establish translations or validated replies. Its [model card](https://huggingface.co/EarthSpeciesProject/NatureLM-audio) describes CC-BY-NC-SA model weights, an 8B language model backbone, and out-of-scope tasks. Code, model, datasets and base model may carry different terms. Not bundled; size, inference requirements, rights, audio privacy and hallucination risk need investigation before any optional integration.
2. [ZF-AIM zebra finch study](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.02.12.705387v3) describes species-specific experimental real-time generated call interactions in a controlled research setting. It does not show language translation, safety, generalization to pets/crows/cows/lizards, or a phone-ready reply generator. The methods and animal-welfare oversight are not interchangeable with casual outdoor playback.
3. [BirdNET Analyzer](https://github.com/birdnet-team/BirdNET-Analyzer) classifies bird vocalizations; review the exact model/version and permitted use separately from code licensing. The archived [BirdNET-Lite](https://github.com/birdnet-team/BirdNET-Lite) model is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Species guesses are not meaning or individual identity; performance is location- and sound-dependent. Not bundled here. Check current availability, acceptable-use rules, version, mobile size and licensing before integration.
4. [Google YAMNet](https://github.com/tensorflow/models/tree/master/research/audioset/yamnet) predicts 521 general AudioSet events. The included Lite version is offline and relatively small. It includes broad dog, cat, cattle, crow, bird, rodent, insect and frog classes, but no dedicated gecko category and no intent classifier. The prototype's 0.25 animal score and 0.45 competing sound gate are uncalibrated conservative UI heuristics, not accuracy claims. Three independent windows are merged by maximum; multiple related guesses can refer to the same sound. The model may be wrong or miss quiet/distant sounds.
5. [Rat ultrasonic study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27746726/) reports different rat call families, including high ultrasonic vocalizations with context-dependent roles. A 16 kHz capture pipeline cannot represent a 22–50 kHz ultrasonic signal. Ordinary phones' microphones and speakers cannot be assumed to handle that range. A capable external ultrasonic microphone/speaker and a validated species-specific model would need a separate design, safety, permission and hardware tests.
6. [Gecko sound research](https://doi.org/10.1093/iob/obz014) shows vocal variation among geckos; many lizards use other modalities. A generic bird chirp result is not evidence of a gecko or its intent. Ants rely heavily on chemicals and touch; a phone mic is not a pheromone sensor.
7. [Ethical playback guidance](https://rochesterbirding.org/conservation/ethical-birding/birdsong/) explains that recorded calls can change behavior and restrictions differ by place. No automatic playback, loops, loudness changes, claims of positive meanings, or near-nest encouragement. Manual sample replay is explicitly labelled not a reply and warns about wildlife and human voices. This is not a blanket claim that a playback is safe.

## Next steps before any claims or release

- Test on labelled, consented, appropriately licensed animal recordings representative of South Indian environments, held-out species, distance, traffic, wind and overlapping speech; report false positives/negatives and unknown rates rather than only examples. Do not commit personal audio.
- Confirm actual microphone and speaker capability on target phones, screen-off/interruptions, permission denial, lifecycle cancellation, CPU, battery, memory and headphones; no background recording.
- For a species-specific reply, obtain an independently validated model, approved licence and provenance, species/context data, an animal welfare review and measured playback hardware. Showing an acoustic imitation as a verified reply is not acceptable. Do not infer distress/hunger/meaning from a sound alone.
- Review native dependencies, compiled permission set, third-party notices, signing, safety and a real user/device test before proposing public distribution. Any cloud or upload requires separate explicit consent, cost and privacy review. Current build is offline and has no service or upload.
