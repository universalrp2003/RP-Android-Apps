# Privacy policy — RP Ideas Android apps

**Last updated: 29 October 2026** · **Developer: Ramesh prathap .R — universalrp2003@gmail.com**

This policy covers RP Ideas Android apps distributed through the official website and their online features. App versions and feature availability vary; older app versions and unreleased builds may behave differently.

## The short version

The developer does not operate an app backend, account system, advertising network, analytics or crash-reporting service. The developer does not receive or see data on your phone. Each app stores its own settings and local content on the device. When you choose an online feature, your phone connects directly to the relevant provider; that provider receives the request and ordinary connection data such as your IP address under its own privacy policy.

**RP Vocal Remover Studio processes selected audio on-device. It has no selected-audio upload path and does not use an external audio-processing service.** Its model may be downloaded from GitHub and verified before use. Non-Store app update checks do not transmit audio or other app content.

## Daily website update notices

Non-Store apps use a minimal Internet connection to check public release metadata at most once per rolling 24 hours when the app is opened or resumed. A notice appears only when a newer stable version is found. Checks require the app to be used, connectivity, and available valid release metadata; they are not guaranteed to run or succeed. The check reads public metadata from the official website and, when needed, GitHub release metadata. It does not send SMS, transaction or ledger entries, selected audio, messages, locations, backups, passwords, or other app content. The website/GitHub provider may receive ordinary request metadata such as the device IP address and user-agent under its own privacy policy. Tapping Update opens the relevant official website section in the browser. The user downloads there and proceeds through Android's normal user-confirmed installation flow; non-Store apps do not download or install update APKs themselves. Tamilan Store's own update and installation behavior is separate and unchanged.

## App-by-app data use

### Live Guard (CleanSweep)

- **Local use:** The app reads device, battery, storage, network and permission information to display its phone-care and review features. Settings, favourites, summaries and any enabled histories are stored in app-private storage. AI provider keys and the optional VirusTotal key are encrypted with Android Keystore before being saved; provider keys can be explicitly forgotten in AI settings, and the VirusTotal key can be removed in the malware-check settings. The optional security, charging and mobile-data histories are user-controlled and remain local. All five apps set Android `allowBackup=false`. The four legacy modules also declare explicit rules excluding app-private files, databases and preferences from cloud backup and device transfer where Android honors those rules; OEM transfer behavior may differ, so review the device's system backup settings as well. Clearing in-app history, clearing app data or uninstalling removes the app-private records; Android controls the lifecycle of the Keystore key. Files exported or changed outside the app remain subject to the chosen destination and Android's storage rules.
- **File cleanup:** All-files access is used to find and review files. The app does not delete files without the user's selection and confirmation.
- **Optional AI and assistant:** When you request an AI analysis, chat answer, or an enabled daily brief, the selected provider receives the question and the phone-care details included in that request. Depending on your sharing choices, this can include device readings and findings, app names or package/service identifiers, available scan results, and network observations. The app provides a “see exactly what was sent” view for the analysis request. Do not include secrets in prompts. Requests go from the phone to the provider you chose; the developer does not proxy them. Provider retention and use are governed by that provider's policy.
- **Other online tools:** User-started malware-hash lookups send APK SHA-256 hashes, not APK files, to MalwareBazaar and optionally VirusTotal when configured. Public-IP, ping and network-quality features contact the selected public endpoints; a speed test sends test traffic only. News refreshes may request public RSS/Atom feeds, including Google News. When the optional **Rain & cyclone alert** feature is turned on, the app sends either the device's cached/quick location (when device location is enabled and permitted) or the user's saved fallback coordinates to Open-Meteo and GDACS public cyclone feeds (and optionally to Tomorrow.io when the user supplies a personal Android-Keystore-encrypted API key), and queries Android's Geocoder, Open-Meteo Geocoding, or the public India PIN directory (`api.postalpincode.in`) when searching a place or PIN code. No location or weather request goes to the developer. These hosts receive their normal request and connection metadata. If you use online text-to-speech, the selected TTS provider may receive the text being spoken. Voice input uses Android's speech-recognition flow; Android's recognizer may process audio under its own provider settings and privacy policy. Live Guard does not save recordings or upload microphone audio to the developer.
- **Permissions:** Depending on the feature and Android version, Live Guard may request all-files access, notifications, location (Android requires it to expose certain Wi-Fi/cell information), phone-state access, usage access, or display-over-other-apps access. Permissions are used for the feature described in the app and can be reviewed or revoked in Android Settings. Live Guard does not use an Accessibility service or notification-listener service and does not request SMS, contacts, call-log or device-admin access.

### Ramesh Radio

- Station audio streams directly from the station selected by the user. HTTPS is preferred when a station offers it; legacy HTTP streams remain available for compatibility. HTTP audio is not encrypted in transit, so other parties on the network may be able to observe or alter the stream. The station and hosting provider receive the connection and normal network metadata.
- The bundled station list is available offline. A public Radio-Browser directory search is made only when the user starts a search; the directory receives the search terms and normal connection metadata.
- Local songs are selected and played on-device. The app does not upload local audio. Audio/media permissions or Android's file picker are used to choose local files.
- **Strictly optional Mobile Tower permission (`ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION`):** Because Android classifies Mobile Signal Tower town lookup inside the system `Location` permission group, `Ramesh Radio` never requests Location permission automatically on launch, when opening Rain Settings, or when checking forecasts. Saving a 6-digit PIN code or town/village works with Location permission completely turned off (`Don't allow`). `ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION` is requested only if the user explicitly taps **`Enable Mobile Tower (Optional)`** inside **Rain & Cyclone Voice Alert**, never uses GPS (`0%` GPS drain), never tracks or stores movement history, and can be revoked at any time in Android App Permissions.

### PulseEQ

- A once-daily foreground release check reads public metadata from the official website (with GitHub release metadata as a fallback). It sends no audio; the provider may receive ordinary request metadata such as the device IP address. A newer version opens the official website in the browser; PulseEQ does not download or install APKs.
- Whether effects apply outside PulseEQ depends on Android audio sessions and the device/ROM. System-wide processing is not guaranteed.

### Daily Vault

- **Local data:** Manual spending records, notes, the encrypted vault and exported backup contents remain on the device or at the user-selected backup destination. The app does not upload ledger, note, password or backup data.
- **Update metadata:** INTERNET is used only for the once-daily foreground public release-metadata check described above. No SMS permission is requested. The official website/GitHub may receive ordinary request metadata such as the device IP address. Update actions open the official website in the browser; the app does not download or install APKs.

### Daily Vault / தினக்கணக்கு (0.5)

- Manual spending and notes stay in app-private, atomically written encrypted storage. No SMS, contacts, microphone, location, advertising SDK or app-opening biometric permission. An Android-Keystore-wrapped local credential enables automatic opening without a login password. Device-key/app-data loss can make local records unreadable; a separate exported backup is needed for recovery.
- There is no app-opening authentication gate in0.5, by owner choice. Screenshots, recordings and recent-app previews are permitted. Anyone using the unlocked phone can view/capture records. Use the phone's screen lock. An old password-only file may require its previous password once to recover existing records, without silently erasing them.
- Export explicitly asks for a backup password and confirmation. The portable TDV-v1 backup uses AES-GCM and PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 independently of the device-local credential. Restore requires that file's password and explicit acknowledgement that current records will be replaced, not merged. The whole archive is authenticated/validated before replacement. No forgotten-password recovery; keep the file and password separately and test restore. Export provider failures may leave incomplete files. Automatic Android backup is disabled; clearing app data/uninstalling may erase records.
- INTERNET is only for the existing daily foreground public release-metadata check. Records, notes, passwords and backup contents are never included. The official site/GitHub may receive normal connection metadata such as IP. Update actions open the external browser; Daily Vault does not download/install APKs. User-selected backup files go to the Android document provider chosen by the user; a cloud document provider handles that encrypted file under its own policy.

- **Update metadata:** INTERNET is used only for the once-daily foreground public release-metadata check described above. ### Oor Arivippu (Place Announcer)

- **Location and tracking:** When the user starts a trip, the app reads location from Android's location services. Foreground location is required; background location is a separate optional Android permission for screen-off tracking. The app processes current readings on-device for place announcements and distance checks against the selected destination. It does not send location to a developer-operated backend or receive it at the developer.
- **Geocoder lookups:** The app passes coordinates to Android's Geocoder to resolve place names and passes a user-entered place name to the Geocoder when setting a destination. Depending on the Android/OEM Geocoder implementation, a system or provider service may perform the lookup online and receive relevant coordinates or search text under that provider's privacy policy. This behavior is outside the app developer's control and can vary by device. If validated internet is unavailable, Oor Arivippu pauses new place/district/highway and destination announcements; GPS tracking can continue.
- **Voice output:** Spoken place and destination alerts use the Android Text-to-Speech engine selected on the phone. The user can choose from up to four Tamil or English voices exposed by that engine, including voices Android marks as network-dependent. If a network voice is selected, its TTS provider may receive the text being spoken; handling is controlled by that provider. The app does not operate a speech server.
- **Offline reminders:** The user may choose Off, 5, 10, 15 or 30 minutes (15-minute default) for reminders while tracking without internet. The reminder says that internet is unavailable and that place/destination speech is paused. It uses a locally available on-device TTS voice or a short local beep; the app does not use an online voice service for the reminder.
- **Local data and permissions:** Preferences, the selected destination name/coordinates and a bounded announcement history are stored in app-private storage. The app requests foreground location, notifications as required by Android, internet/network state, and optionally background location. Release notices open the official website in the browser; the app does not download or install update APKs. Oor Arivippu does not request microphone, contacts, phone or call-log permissions.
- **Rain & cyclone alerts (Live Guard, Ramesh Radio, and Oor Arivippu):** When enabled, the app sends only the selected coordinates (live location in Live Guard / Oor Arivippu, connected mobile cell-tower approximate location `ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION` when permitted in Ramesh Radio, or a user-saved PIN/village fallback when Location is off) directly over HTTPS to Tomorrow.io's keyless `/v4/timelines` 1-minute radar endpoint by default (or authenticated with an optional personal API key encrypted locally using Android Keystore), with automatic fallback to MET Norway (`api.met.no`), Open-Meteo, and GDACS public cyclone feeds if rate-limited. Optional 6-digit Indian PIN lookups query the public India Post postal directory and Open-Meteo geocoding. Ramesh Radio checks forecasts strictly while its window is open or radio audio is actively playing, Oor Arivippu strictly while its announcer is active or window is open, and Live Guard supports optional 24×7 background alerts. No location or weather history is sent to the developer.

### RP Vocal Remover Studio

- Selected songs are processed on-device. The original is left unchanged; the first pass creates an instrumental WAV. A second vocal-reduction pass is optional and user-started. A separate 320 kb/s CBR MP3 export is optional; MP3 is lossy and the WAV is retained.
- The model is downloaded from the upstream GitHub release and checked against its pinned checksum. GitHub receives normal download request metadata. The model download is not an audio upload.
- The app does not send the selected audio to the developer, GitHub, an AI provider or an audio-processing service. Output files are written to the location selected by the user and remain subject to Android storage and backup settings.

### SMS Guard (குறுஞ்செய்தி காவலன்)

- **Local records and permission choices:** SMS Guard reads stored/incoming SMS for inbox, bill and finance processing through Android's SMS provider. App-private link verdicts, ledger entries and account labels stay on-device (`allowBackup=false`, cloud backup/transfer excluded); ordinary preferences/records are app-private, not additionally encrypted by this module. The optional Safe Browsing API key and Finance credential hash use separate AES-GCM storage backed by Android Keystore. Initial companion setup explains SMS read/receive and notifications only. Contacts for saved names and Phone for SIM labels are separate optional actions; SMS sending permission is requested on Send, with no automatic send after granting. Incoming MMS is incomplete; new default-SMS setup is unavailable. Retain a full default messaging application.
- **Online link checks (Internet toggle):** Local rules run on-device. With Internet Access ON, URLs extracted from non-OTP messages can be sent in header-only redirect probes; the destination/redirect services receive the URL (including any embedded identifiers) and normal IP/request metadata. Hostnames are resolved using Android's configured DNS/Private DNS and checked through Cloudflare's security DNS-over-HTTPS hostname (`security.cloudflare-dns.com`). These services receive the relevant domain/request metadata; the app does not send the entire SMS body to them. If the user configures an optional Google Safe Browsing key, final URLs, the API key and request metadata are sent to `safebrowsing.googleapis.com` for that check. System DNS is not bypassed with raw-IP requests. Failed/offline checks remain Pending; failures invalidate cached safe results. Internet OFF blocks subsequent link-verification requests; it cannot recall an already issued request. Link checks are not a safety guarantee.
- **Cloud HD voice is optional and separately consented:** Beginning in 1.1.15, cloud speech consent defaults OFF even on upgrades; the Internet switch alone is not consent. A prominent Tamil/English choice explains that enabling it transmits spoken announcement text to Microsoft's Edge Read-Aloud service (`speech.platform.bing.com` over TLS), including background SMS speech. Text may include sender/contact names, ordinary SMS content, transaction amounts and summaries. No complete contact list or finance database is uploaded by this feature, but sensitive information in the spoken text can leave the device. Microsoft receives request/network metadata and handles data under its [privacy statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement); no particular provider retention/deletion period is promised. This corrects the previous inaccurate blanket claim that all SMS-derived content never went to a cloud backend.
- **Speech screening and limits:** Complete sent/ordinary bodies are screened before shortening, and a common final boundary screens all speech. Detected OTP/PIN/password-bearing text is suppressed; labelled account/masked and other 4+ digit numbers are redacted while explicitly labelled bounded amounts may remain. Heuristics can miss novel formats, unlabelled words or misleading labels and can silence innocent messages. They are not a universal secret detector or guarantee that every secret is removed. Do not enable cloud sharing for content you cannot share. Local and cloud announcements can be heard by nearby people; CallGuard attempts to silence speech during calls.
- **Decline, fallback, withdrawal and cache:** Without cloud consent plus Internet access and connectivity, only an installed system voice reported by Android as non-network is permitted. No such voice means no speech, not silent network fallback. A chosen network-backed Android voice requires the same opt-in; its own TTS provider's policy applies, not necessarily Microsoft's. The app relies on Android/engine voice flags and cannot certify a third-party engine's implementation. Controls permits consent review/withdrawal. Withdrawal or Internet OFF stops current playback and prevents later cloud sends/retries; already transmitted text cannot be recalled. Recent HD MP3 clips are cached in app-private cache with a bounded clip count, not a guaranteed expiry time. Withdrawal deletes this audio cache; legacy audio cache is invalidated on migration. This does not erase SMS, bills, accounts or finance records. The cache is not a separate encrypted voice vault.
- **Finance lock:** Finance calculations stay on-device except for spoken summary text explicitly opted into cloud speech as described above. Biometrics use Android's local `BiometricPrompt`; the app does not receive biometric templates. A custom Finance PIN/password uses a SHA-256 hash with a fixed application prefix (not a random per-user salt or a slow password KDF), encrypted using an Android-Keystore-backed AES-GCM key. This UI lock does not separately encrypt the full finance database.
- **Installation warning and audit:** The owner reported Play Protect's sensitive-data fraud-protection block. SMS read/receive declarations remain necessary and installation may still be blocked. No Google clearance is claimed. Keep protection enabled and respect warnings; do not uninstall/reset to force installation. [Warning, limitations and source-audit access](docs/sms-guard-play-protect.md). Public source enables inspection, not a safety certification.

## Release checks and APK installation

Non-Store apps check public stable-release metadata at most once per rolling 24 hours when opened or resumed. Checks depend on app use, connectivity and available release metadata and are not guaranteed to succeed. The checks do not include app content; the website/GitHub provider may receive ordinary request metadata such as the device IP address. When a newer stable release is found, the notice opens the relevant official website section in the browser. The user downloads the APK there and follows Android's normal user-confirmed installation flow. The app itself does not download or install update APKs. Tamilan Store's own update/install behavior is separate and unchanged. These apps do not bypass Play Protect or Android's signature checks.

## Data retention and deletion

- Settings, credentials and locally generated records stay within each app's storage unless you explicitly export or share them. Credential storage is local to the app; users should still protect their device and avoid sharing screenshots or exports that contain secrets.
- Optional Live Guard histories remain until cleared, app data is removed, or the app is uninstalled. Turning a history off stops new collection but does not necessarily erase existing records; use the relevant Clear control to delete them.
- Radio favourites, Oor Arivippu settings, its selected destination and its announcement history remain local. Oor history can be cleared in the app; clearing app data or uninstalling also removes its private preferences. Radio audio files and vocal-removal outputs are in locations chosen through Android and are not removed by uninstalling the app unless Android or the user removes them.
- Online providers may retain requests under their own terms and retention policies. To ask a provider about its records, contact that provider; the app developer has no copy to delete.

## Children

These general-purpose utilities are not designed specifically for children. The apps do not knowingly collect personal data for the developer.
