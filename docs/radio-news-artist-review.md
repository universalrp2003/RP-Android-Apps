# Radio news and artist search review

This is a static catalogue/name review, not a listening, rights, identity, or availability certification. Station URLs and IDs are retained. A directory tag alone is not evidence of a news-focused schedule, and artist-name results may be partial or unrelated; no replacement streams have been invented.

## Tamil news

Six entries previously appeared under Tamil news solely from community tags. They have been moved to Tamil/World Tamil browsing rather than deleted: AIR Tuticorin (general public radio), Sooriyan FM Sri Lanka (general station), Shakthi FM (music and entertainment with news), Universal Health Radio Tamil (health education), Punnagai Radio and Siragugal FM (music/entertainment tags). The Shakthi broadcaster itself describes music, news and entertainment: https://shakthifm.com/. These stations may have scheduled bulletins; that does not make them continuous news feeds.

No independently confirmed direct Tamil-news audio stream was established in this review. The News page links to the broadcasters' own websites: https://tamil.news18.com/live-tv/ and https://www.polimernews.com/dlive-tv, plus https://www.bbc.com/tamil. These are external web pages, not in-app radio streams; availability and content are controlled by the publishers. Community directory searches remain clearly unverified and may include general stations.
