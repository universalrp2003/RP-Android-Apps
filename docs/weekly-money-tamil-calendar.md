# Tamil loan-date display — bounded civil table

Offline table covers **15 January–31 December 2026 only**. It is not an astronomical calendar, festival predictor, or a fixed Gregorian month/day rule repeated across years. Outside its range the UI explicitly says the calendar date is unavailable; it never guesses or changes the original Gregorian loan date. More years remain required for universal coverage.

Source: https://nivacalendar.in/tamil/2026 (Tamil months beginning in 2026). This differs from some generic solar-calendar sites by one day for Thai, Aavani and Purattasi; those generic tables are not used.

Cross-checks:
- https://www.prokerala.com/general/calendar/tamilcalendar.php?year=2025&mon=thai&la=ta — Thai 2026 starts 15 January (URL year represents Tamil-year start).
- https://www.prokerala.com/general/calendar/tamilcalendar.php?year=2026&mon=aavani&la=ta — 18 August–17 September.
- https://www.prokerala.com/general/calendar/tamilcalendar.php?year=2026&mon=aippasi&la=ta — 18 October–16 November.
- https://www.prokerala.com/astrology/tamil-panchangam/2026-november-12.html — 12 November = Aippasi 26.
- https://calendar.goldenchennai.com/valarpirai-dates/valarpirai-dates-in-november-2026/ — 17 November = Karthigai 1, 19 November = Karthigai 3.

These dates are display metadata only, never the basis for receipt numbering, deadlines, automatic holidays or financial calculations.
