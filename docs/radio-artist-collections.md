# Artist collections and SS Tamil possible alternative

Artist shortcuts originally searched station names; they did not contain a playlist or prove that a named artist was on air. Some returned zero or unrelated matches. The artist results now offer a clearly labelled external Spotify page where the seven specific mappings below were independently checked as pages. They open only on a deliberate tap, not in the radio player; no audio is copied, downloaded, embedded or claimed to be playable without Spotify. A Spotify account, app or subscription may be required; licensing, regional availability and catalogue changes are controlled by Spotify. No playback or account test was performed. Other artists still only search radio-station names and are not represented as having collections.

A second existing catalogue entry called `SS Tamil Radio` at `https://cast6.asurahosting.com/proxy/sstamilradio/stream` returned audio metadata in that audit. A similar name and audio metadata do **not** prove identity, rights, or current playback. The UI only offers it as a possible alternative after an error and starts it only if the user explicitly taps it. Both records, their identifiers and existing favourites are retained; no silent fallback or provider hold bypass is used. Other failed stations remain unresolved and available to retry.
