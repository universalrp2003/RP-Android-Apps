The last six completed calculator entries are now saved on the device and reappear after closing and reopening the app. C starts a new calculation without removing those entries. The current unfinished calculation is not restored. Arithmetic runs left to right.

About contains a bilingual guide to creating, moving, renaming and permanently deleting pages, editing rows, settings and the calculator. The owner confirmed the earlier 1.2 guide and asked for persistent history and publication; this 1.3 change was tested on an Android emulator, not yet on the owner's phone. Eleven native tests passed. A signed in-place upgrade from 1.2 preserved synthetic notes and totals, and a calculation remained visible after force-stop/relaunch and C. The release uses the exact tested signed APK.

**No backup or sync:** uninstalling, clearing app data, resetting or losing the phone removes notes and history. Update over the existing app without uninstalling or clearing data. Previous history from older versions was not saved and cannot be recovered.
